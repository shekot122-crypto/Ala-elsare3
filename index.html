Privacy Policy

We gather data when you register, submit a request, buy services, react to an overview, or fill out a form. You may be asked for your name, email address or phone number. You may also visit the site anonymously.

How do we protect your information?

Sensitive payment data is sent through Stripe. After an exchange, your private data (cards, social security numbers, financials) is not stored on our servers.

Do we disclose information to outside parties?

We do not sell, trade or transfer personally identifiable information to outside parties. This excludes trusted third parties who help us operate the site and agree to keep it confidential. We may release information when needed to follow the law, enforce site policies, or protect rights, property or safety.

COPPA compliance

We do not collect information from anyone under 13. Our site, products and services are directed at people who are at least 13 years old.

Changes to our Privacy Policy

If we change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We may use cookies or other tracking technologies when you visit our website, mobile website or mobile application to help customize the site and improve your experience.

How we use them

To keep the site working (for example, remembering you are logged in), to keep your account secure, and to understand how the site is used so we can improve speed and reliability.

Third parties
